Event Algorithm Design — Canonical Question List

Q1. Which layer(s) does this event touch?
(Domain, Topology, State)

Q2. What is the minimal direct mutation caused by this event?
(Only what the event explicitly changes)

Q3. What invariants must remain true immediately after this mutation?
(Domain and simulation constraints)

Q4. What consequences must be derived from this mutation?
(Not declared by the event itself)

Q5. What structural or topological queries are required to compute those consequences?

Q6. What prioritization, ordering, or selection rules are required during processing?

Q7. Which data structures are therefore required to support those operations efficiently?

Q8. What is the termination condition for processing this event?

Q9. What outputs must be produced and stored after processing this event?
